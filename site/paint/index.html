Image editor

Paint

Open a picture, draw on it, save it. A simple image editor that fits in one window.

  • Download

What it does

  • Draw. Pencil, brush, eraser, fill, text, lines, curves and shapes.
  • Select. Move, resize, delete, or crop to a selection.
  • Transform. Flip, rotate, resize, stretch and skew.
  • Undo. A deep history, not three steps.
  • Files. PNG, JPEG, BMP, GIF, TIFF and more.
  • Private. Works offline. No account, no telemetry.

Paint edits one flat image. Layers are not there yet.

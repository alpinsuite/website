Batch image resizer

Shrink

Make a folder of images small enough to send. Say “under 2 MB, no wider than 1600 pixels” and Shrink does the rest.

  • Coming soon

What it does

  • Batch. Drop files or whole folders on the window.
  • Set the goal, not the knobs. A file size, a maximum width, a format.
  • Before and after. Side by side, at the real output size.
  • Honest about size. A file that cannot meet the budget is reported, never quietly shipped oversized.
  • Safe. Never upscales. Never overwrites an original.
  • Private. Works offline. No account, no telemetry.
